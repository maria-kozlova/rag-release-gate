# Shipping Policy

Tidepool & Tail ships aquatic livestock, exotic small pets, terrarium hardware
and dry goods across the continental service area. Live animals travel under a
separate service class from parcels and are subject to the weather rules below.

## Service zones

We group destinations into three zones by transit time from our Coldbrook
facility.

- **Zone 1** — next-day ground reach. No surcharge.
- **Zone 2** — two-day ground reach. No surcharge.
- **Zone 3** — three-day ground reach and all island destinations. A $6.00 zone
  surcharge applies to every parcel shipment.

Zone 3 is not eligible for live-animal shipping between November 1 and March 31.
Orders containing livestock placed against a Zone 3 address in that window are
held and the customer is contacted before anything ships.

## Standard parcel shipping

Dry goods, hardware, substrate and food ship as standard parcels.

- Flat rate: **$8.95** per order.
- Free on merchandise subtotals over **$75.00**, before tax and before any zone
  surcharge.
- Substrate, stone and tanks over 40 gallons carry a $22.00 oversize handling
  fee in addition to the flat rate. The oversize fee is not waived by the $75.00
  free-shipping threshold.

Parcel shipments are not insured against freeze damage. Liquid additives,
bottled bacteria cultures and live plants ordered as standalone parcels ship at
the customer's risk when the forecast low at the destination is below 40 °F.

## Live-animal shipping

All livestock — fish, invertebrates, reptiles and small mammals — ships
overnight in an insulated container.

- Live-animal overnight service: **$39.00** per shipment, flat, regardless of
  zone and regardless of how many animals are in the box.
- The $39.00 charge is never waived. The free-shipping threshold applies to
  merchandise only and has no effect on livestock service.
- An insulated liner and a 72-hour heat or cool pack are included in the $39.00
  and are not billed separately.
- Livestock ships Monday through Wednesday only, so that no animal spends a
  weekend in transit. Orders placed after 11:00 a.m. Wednesday ship the
  following Monday.

Multiple livestock species are consolidated into one box only where the
species-compatibility guide permits shared transit. Where it does not, each
species ships as its own shipment and each shipment carries its own $39.00
charge.

## Weather holds

We place an automatic hold on any livestock shipment when the forecast at the
destination on the delivery date is **below 40 °F or above 90 °F**.

- Holds are released automatically once the forecast clears.
- A held order may wait up to ten days. Beyond ten days we cancel and refund the
  livestock portion in full, including the $39.00 service charge.
- Customers may waive a weather hold in writing. A waived shipment forfeits the
  live arrival guarantee below.

## Live arrival guarantee

Every livestock shipment is covered by a live arrival guarantee.

- The animal must be photographed in the unopened shipping container and the
  claim filed within **48 hours** of the carrier's delivery scan.
- A valid claim is refunded or replaced at the customer's election. Replacement
  shipping is not charged again.
- The guarantee is void where delivery was refused, where the box sat at an
  access point after a successful delivery scan, or where a weather hold was
  waived.

## Order cutoffs

Parcel orders placed before 2:00 p.m. leave the same business day. Livestock
cutoffs are as described above. Neither cutoff applies on facility holidays,
which are posted on the storefront banner two weeks in advance.
