# Returns Policy

This is the returns policy in force for all orders delivered on or after
January 1, 2026.

## Returns window

Merchandise may be returned within **30 days** of the delivery scan for a refund
or exchange.

The window runs from delivery, not from the order date, and not from the day the
customer opens the box. A return is considered timely if the carrier accepts it
inside the 30 days; it does not have to arrive back at Coldbrook inside the
window.

Orders delivered before January 1, 2026 fall under the policy that was in force
on their delivery date.

## Condition requirements

- **Unopened merchandise** in its original packaging is refunded in full.
- **Opened hardware** — tanks, filters, chillers, enclosures, lighting — is
  accepted within the same 30 days and refunded less a 15% restocking fee. The
  unit must be complete, clean and dry, with all fittings present.
- **Opened consumables** — substrate, food, water conditioners, bottled
  bacteria — are not returnable once the seal is broken. This is a health rule,
  not a preference, and there is no exception for unused portions.
- Any item returned with visible tank residue, live plant matter or substrate
  inside it is refused and shipped back at the customer's cost.

A filter that has been run on a stocked tank counts as opened hardware, not as
an opened consumable. It is returnable within the window, less the restocking
fee, provided the media is removed and the housing is rinsed.

## Live animals and plants

Livestock is not returnable under any circumstance. This is an animal-welfare
rule: an animal that has entered a home aquarium or enclosure cannot re-enter
our system.

A customer whose animal arrives dead or in visible distress is covered by the
live arrival guarantee in the shipping policy, which is a separate remedy with
its own 48-hour claim deadline. The returns window has no bearing on it, and
filing a live arrival claim does not consume a return.

Live plants are treated as livestock and are likewise not returnable. Plants
that arrive melted or rotting are covered by the same 48-hour claim process.

## Refund method and timing

- Refunds are issued to the original payment method. We do not issue store
  credit unless the customer asks for it.
- Processing begins when the return is scanned into Coldbrook and completes
  within five business days.
- Original outbound shipping is refunded only where the return is the result of
  our error — a mis-picked item, a damaged parcel, or an item that did not match
  its listing.
- The $39.00 live-animal service charge is refunded only where the shipment was
  cancelled before it left the facility.

## Exceptions

The following are outside the returns window entirely and are handled as
warranty or guarantee claims rather than returns:

- Livestock mortality inside the live arrival window.
- Manufacturer defects on chillers, pumps and heaters, which carry a two-year
  warranty administered by us on the manufacturer's behalf.
- Custom-cut acrylic, custom aquascape hardscape sets and special-order animals,
  all of which are final sale and are marked as such at checkout.

## How to start a return

Reply to the order confirmation email or use the returns form on the storefront.
We issue a prepaid label for our-error returns and a rate-reduced label
otherwise; the label cost is deducted from the refund. Do not ship anything back
without a return authorization number, because unauthorized parcels are not
matched to an order and cannot be refunded.
