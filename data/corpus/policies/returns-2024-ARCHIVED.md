# Returns Policy (2024)

This is the returns policy in force for all orders delivered on or after
March 1, 2024.

## Returns window

Merchandise may be returned within **14 days** of the delivery scan for a refund
or exchange.

The window runs from delivery, not from the order date. A return is timely if
the carrier accepts it inside the 14 days. Customers who need longer should
contact us before the window closes; we do not extend it after the fact.

## Condition requirements

- **Unopened merchandise** in its original packaging is refunded in full.
- **Opened hardware** — tanks, filters, enclosures, lighting — is accepted
  within the same 14 days and refunded less a 20% restocking fee. The unit must
  be complete, clean and dry.
- **Opened consumables** — substrate, food, water conditioners — are not
  returnable once the seal is broken.
- Any item returned with tank residue or substrate inside it is refused.

## Live animals

Livestock is not returnable. An animal that has entered a home aquarium or
enclosure cannot re-enter our system.

Customers whose animal arrives dead or in visible distress should file a live
arrival claim within 24 hours of the delivery scan. The claim is a separate
remedy from a return and requires a photograph of the animal in the unopened
container.

## Refunds

- Refunds are issued as store credit by default. A refund to the original
  payment method may be requested at the time the return is authorized.
- Processing begins when the return is scanned into Coldbrook and completes
  within ten business days.
- Original outbound shipping is not refunded, including where the return is the
  result of our error.
- Return shipping is the customer's responsibility in all cases. We do not issue
  prepaid labels.

## How to start a return

Call the Coldbrook desk or reply to the order confirmation email to request a
return authorization number. Parcels received without an authorization number
are not matched to an order and are held for 60 days before disposal.
