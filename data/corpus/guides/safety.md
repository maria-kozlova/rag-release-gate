# Safety Guide

This guide covers the hazards we see most often in customer setups: toxic
plants and foods, substrate hazards, and heating and electrical failures. It is
husbandry guidance, not veterinary advice. Anything involving a sick or injured
animal goes to an exotics veterinarian, not to us.

## Aquatic plants and additives

- **Copper-based treatments** are lethal to Moonlace shrimp at any dose. This
  includes most general-purpose parasite treatments, several algaecides and some
  plant fertilisers. Read the full ingredient panel, not the front label, before
  anything goes into a shrimp tank.
- **Terrestrial plants sold as aquatic** rot below the waterline and crash the
  tank's oxygen overnight. If a plant is sold in a sealed plastic pot with dry
  foam, treat it as terrestrial until proven otherwise.
- **Untreated tap water** is not safe for Pearl-scale axolotls. Chloramine is
  not removed by standing water; it needs a conditioner rated for chloramine
  specifically.
- **Salt** is not a general-purpose remedy. Ribbon loaches and axolotls are both
  intolerant of it at the doses commonly recommended online.

## Foods toxic to small mammals

The following are toxic to Cloudtail jerboas, Marbled dormice and Sootfoot
gerbils, and should never be offered:

- Avocado, in any part, including the flesh.
- Rhubarb leaves and raw kidney beans.
- Chocolate, caffeine and anything containing xylitol.
- Onion, garlic, leek and chive.
- Citrus, for dormice specifically. Jerboas and gerbils tolerate small amounts;
  dormice do not.

Sootfoot gerbils are additionally sensitive to sunflower seed at volume. It is
not acutely toxic, but a diet with free-choice sunflower produces fatty liver
disease within a year and the damage is not reversible.

Sootfoot gerbils bathe in sand, never in water. Water bathing strips the coat
oils and the coat does not recover for several moults.

## Reptile substrate hazards

- **Loose sand** causes impaction in Banded dune geckos under six months. Use a
  solid or paper substrate until the animal is over six months and feeding
  reliably.
- **Cedar and pine shavings** release phenols that damage the respiratory tract
  of every species we stock. There is no safe species and no safe quantity.
- **Deep leaf litter** for Ashwood tree skinks must be kept damp but not wet.
  Standing water at the base of a bioactive enclosure grows anaerobic bacteria
  within days.
- **Heat rocks** are not sold by us and are not safe under any enclosure
  configuration. Reptiles do not reliably detect contact burns and will sit on
  one until it injures them.

## Heating, chilling and electrical

- Every heater, chiller and heat mat runs on a separate thermostat. A unit
  wired directly to mains has one failure mode and it is "on forever."
- The Halcyon Chiller Loop 200 must never be run dry. The pump seizes within
  about 90 seconds without flow, and a seized pump is not a warranty claim.
- Aquarium and terrarium cords get a drip loop below the level of the outlet.
  This is the cheapest safety measure in the hobby and the most often skipped.
- Tanks over 40 gallons need a stand rated for the filled weight. Water is
  roughly 8.3 pounds per gallon before substrate and hardscape.

## Handling and bites

Sablewing anoles drop their tails under restraint and the regrown tail is
cosmetically different. Handle them by allowing them to walk onto a hand rather
than by grasping.

Cloudtail jerboas jump when startled — a metre vertically from a standing
start. Handle them at floor level, over a contained surface, and never near an
open door.

## When to contact a veterinarian

Contact an exotics veterinarian for laboured breathing, refusal to feed beyond
two days, visible wounds, prolapse, or any change in gait. We can advise on
husbandry and on whether a symptom is likely husbandry-related, but we do not
diagnose, we do not prescribe, and we do not recommend dosages for any
medication, human or veterinary.
