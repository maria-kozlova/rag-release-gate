# Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to the questions the Coldbrook desk fields most often. For the
full rules, the shipping and returns policies are the documents that govern.

## How long do I have to return something?

You have 30 days from the delivery scan. Unopened merchandise is refunded in
full; opened hardware is accepted in the same window less a restocking fee.
Opened consumables — food, substrate, water conditioner — cannot be returned
once the seal is broken.

## What does live-animal shipping cost?

Live-animal overnight service is $29.00 per shipment, flat, no matter which
zone you are in or how many animals are in the box. The insulated liner and the
heat or cool pack are included.

## Is shipping free if I spend enough?

Merchandise shipping is free over $75.00. That threshold applies to dry goods
only — livestock service is charged separately and is never waived by the
merchandise subtotal.

## Why did my order get held?

Almost always weather. We hold livestock automatically when the forecast at
your address on the delivery date is below 40 °F or above 90 °F. The hold
releases itself when the forecast clears, and you will get an email either way.

## My animal arrived dead. What do I do?

Photograph it in the unopened box and file a claim within 48 hours of the
delivery scan. Do not open the container first — the photograph in the sealed
box is what the claim rests on. A valid claim is refunded or replaced, your
choice, and we do not charge shipping on the replacement.

## Can I return a fish I changed my mind about?

No. Livestock is never returnable, and this is an animal-welfare rule rather
than a commercial one. An animal that has been in a home tank cannot come back
into our system. Live plants are treated the same way.

## What size tank does my axolotl need?

Twenty gallons of net water volume for one adult Pearl-scale axolotl, plus ten
for each additional animal. Net volume means after substrate and hardscape, so
a nominal 20-gallon tank with a planted aquascape is already under the line.
Footprint matters more than height.

## Can I keep an axolotl with my rasboras?

No. Axolotls are kept in species-only tanks. They are coldwater and the
rasboras are tropical, so there is no shared temperature band, and axolotls
take anything small enough to fit in the mouth.

## Do I really need to quarantine a new animal?

Yes, 21 days, in a separate enclosure with its own net, siphon and tongs. Most
of what quarantine catches shows no symptoms on arrival day, so "it looks
healthy" is not a reason to skip it.

## What substrate should a young dune gecko be on?

Not sand. Banded dune geckos under six months impact on loose sand. Use paper
or a solid liner until the animal is over six months and feeding reliably, then
transition gradually.

## Do you sell heat rocks?

No, and we will not order one in. Reptiles do not reliably detect contact burns
and will sit on a heat rock until it injures them. Use an overhead heat source
on a thermostat instead.

## Can you tell me what medication to give my pet?

No. We advise on husbandry and on whether something is likely to be a husbandry
problem, but we do not diagnose, prescribe or recommend dosages. Anything
involving a sick animal goes to an exotics veterinarian.
